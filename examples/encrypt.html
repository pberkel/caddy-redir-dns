Encrypt TXT Record

Demo page for caddy-redir-dns — encrypt a redirect target in your browser using AES-256-GCM (WebCrypto API).

Generate a key with: openssl rand -base64 32 — use the same value in the encryption_key directive in your Caddy config.

Same format as a plaintext TXT record: https://example.com or https://example.com permanent

    How it works: A random 12-byte nonce is generated for every encryption.
    The binary blob [nonce || ciphertext || 16B tag] is base64url-encoded for DNS storage.
    Each click produces a different ciphertext — all decrypt to the same plaintext.
    Encryption runs entirely in your browser; neither the key nor the plaintext is sent over the network.
